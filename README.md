# Chris Stauffer

**Computer vision, AI, and software development**
Visionary Systems and Research · Massachusetts

I build systems that make sense of images and video, from tracking objects through a scene to
turning imagery into situational awareness, and I experiment with new AI techniques along the
way.

### Areas of work

- **Computer vision**: detection, classification, and object tracking in video
- **Situational awareness**: long-term understanding of scenes from imagery and video
- **Geo-tagged imagery**: tools for sampling, storing, and viewing imagery with location metadata
- **AI and machine learning**: deep learning experiments and LLM-powered applications
- **Video creation**: systems for assembling and producing video
- **Software development**: Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, and Java

### Stauffer Labs

[Stauffer Labs](https://github.com/stauffer-labs) is where I keep my tools, libraries, and
experiments, built on shared project templates and
